# CareBridge: 2-minute demo script

**Setup before recording:** Chrome, app open at the home screen, volume up, sound recorded from the computer (CareBridge's voice must be audible). Profile cleared (DevTools → Application → Local Storage → delete `carebridge.settings`) so the needs screen shows. Zoom the browser to 125% so text is readable on video.

---

### 0:00–0:15: The problem
**Show:** `samples/bill.png` full screen.
**Say:** "This is a hospital bill. If you're blind, it's unreadable. If you're Deaf, it tells you to call a number. If you have a learning disability or chronic fatigue, it's a wall of jargon. CareBridge turns any medical paper into the format each disabled patient can actually use."

### 0:15–0:30: Profile
**Do:** On the needs screen, tick **Blind or low vision**. Point out that high contrast, large text and auto-read switch on. Set **Voice → Female** (or Male) and click **Preview voice**. Click **Save and continue**.
**Say:** "You tell it once how you need information, even what kind of voice you want. It remembers on your device."

### 0:30–0:40: Load a document
**Do:** Click **Try a sample document → Hospital bill** (or upload a photo if you have a key and time).
**Say:** "I'll use a fictional bill. It reads the document and pulls out what matters."

### 0:40–1:00: Read aloud, then interrupt
**Do:** Let it read the intro and start the "Amount you owe" card. Point to Bridgey in the top right and the caption bubble, then the highlighted sentence. **Mid-sentence, press Q.**
**Say:** "Pick a female or male voice and Bridgey reads it to you, with live captions in the bubble. And if I'm confused, I can interrupt at any moment." *(Audio stops instantly, and Bridgey shows paused.)*

### 1:00–1:20: Ask, get a simple answer with its source
**Do:** Tap **What does this mean?** (or speak "Why do I owe this?" with the mic).
**Say:** "I can type, speak, or tap. The answer comes only from my document, and it shows the exact line it came from, so I can check it." *(Point to the quote.)*

### 1:20–1:30: Resume
**Do:** Click **Continue reading**.
**Say:** "Continue picks up on exactly the same sentence."

### 1:30–1:40: Show original
**Do:** Press Stop. On **Amount you owe ($482.17)**, open **Show original**.
**Say:** "Every card shows the original text. Amounts, dates and medicines are flagged to double-check with a person. It never guesses."

### 1:40–1:52: Switch profile
**Do:** **My needs & settings** → untick Blind, tick **Cognitive or learning** and **Deaf or hard of hearing** → Save.
**Say:** "Same document, different person. Now it's one simple card at a time, with no audio, and instead of 'call this number' it writes a message for me."
**Do:** Click **Next** once, then on the contacts card click **Write a message for me** (skip if no key).

### 1:52–2:00: Why not a general chatbot?
**Say:** "Why not just use a chatbot? CareBridge remembers your accessibility profile, gives consistent accessible output, shows the source text so you can verify it, lets you interrupt mid-explanation and resume, and works with screen readers out of the box. CareBridge."

---

**Backup if the network fails:** everything up to 1:00 works without the AI. "Say that again" and "Say it simpler" also work offline from the cached sample.
